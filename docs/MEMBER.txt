GuildLedger - for guild members
================================

Our guild keeps a log of guild chat. Officers' PCs send it to the officer channels in Discord.
This addon helps them: while you play, it remembers recent guild chat lines, and when an officer's
addon asks for lines it missed, yours sends them over in the background. You never see anything,
it sends nothing to chat, and it needs no Python or extra program.

Install
1. Copy this "GuildLedger" folder into your AddOns folder:
   ...\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\
2. Start WoW and make sure "GuildLedger" is ticked under AddOns at character select. Done.

It keeps at most 2000 lines and nothing older than 3 days, and deletes lines once an officer has them.
"/gl status" shows what it holds. Questions: ask an officer.
