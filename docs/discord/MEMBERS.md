# In the guild's Discord: what members do

For every member. The admin guide is [SERVER.md](SERVER.md). The sections below can be pasted into Discord.

## 1. Get in

Press **Verify** in the welcome channel and press the button the message names. You then get the member role and
a private message from the bot with the next steps. (No private message? It is shown right after the check
instead. Discord: *Privacy Settings → Direct Messages* lets the server's bot write to you.)

## 2. Link your WoW character

1. Press **Get my link code** in the bot's message, or type `/link`.
2. In WoW, on your character:
   - **with the guild's addon pack** (Amazing Forever Guild Pack): type `/gl link CODE` (or paste it in the hub's
     Ledger tab). The code works 30 minutes.
   - **without the addon:** write `!link CODE` in **guild chat**. The code works 10 minutes. The Discord log shows it
     as `!link ••••`.
3. An officer's collector passes it on (after their next `/reload` or logout). The bot then asks you in a private
   message: press **Confirm**. If it names a character you do not play, press **Not me**.
4. Your other characters on the same WoW account are offered by themselves the next time you log in on them
   (with the addon pack).

Afterwards your server nickname becomes your main character, your guild rank gives your role, and you are offered
the role menus. Choose your main with `/main character:`.

## 3. Pick roles

In the roles channel, open a menu (interests, languages) and tick what fits. Pick again at any time; an empty
choice removes that menu's roles.

## 4. Find a character or a member

- `/whois character:` who plays a character (names are suggested while you type)
- right-click a member → **Apps** → **Characters**: all their characters
- `/chars` your own characters; `/seen`, `/joined`, `/activity` for activity
- the character forum: one post per member, titled with their main
- `/craft recipe:` who can craft something and what it takes (start typing, recipes and items are suggested);
  the profession forum lists every crafter and recipe
